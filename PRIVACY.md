# Privacy Policy

**Close Multiple Tabs** is committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection and Usage

- **No Personal Data Collected:** The extension does not collect, log, or track your personal information, browsing history, or identities.
- **Tabs:** The extension only closes the tabs you choose in the current window, when you use its context menu or keyboard shortcuts. Tab information never leaves your browser.
- **No Storage:** The extension does not store any data.
- **No Network Requests:** The extension does not send any network requests.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** There are no third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or telemetry embedded in the extension.
- **Data Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.
